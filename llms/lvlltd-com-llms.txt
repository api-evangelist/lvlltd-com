## Purchase sequence (canonical ESP)

1. GET /api/esp — protocol discovery  
2. GET /api/shop — machine entry  
3. GET /api/catalog?q=… or MCP search_skills  
4. GET /api/esp?skill={id}&phase=evaluate — free evaluation  
5. GET /skills/{id}/outline.json — free outline  
6. GET /api/pay?skill={id} — HTTP 402 (amount, payTo, assetContract)  
7. Transfer maxAmountRequired atomic USDC on Base to payTo  
8. POST /api/pay with X-PAYMENT: {"txHash":"0x…","skill":"{id}"}  
9. Receive sealed_pack.files (bundles return sealed_packs map)  
10. GET /api/proof?skill={id} — only success truth
